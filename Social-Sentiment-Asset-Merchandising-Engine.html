Origin Story

When personal bias quietly derailed a launch decision

During an internal OTT planning meeting, a major movie launch was deprioritized simply because a manager disliked the original novel. It was a classic case of subjective bias masquerading as strategy. The decision ignored cast strength, music, star power, and real audience reactions, relying entirely on one person's taste.

That was the flaw. Movies don’t succeed because a few decision-makers like the source material. They succeed because the audience is talking, reacting, and showing appetite in real time. Rather than relying on individual bias, we can extract that signal directly from the social conversation and turn it into actionable merchandising decisions
