Approach

Led Go-To-Market content promotion across 50+ GEC titles, reality series, and blockbuster films. I combined data-backed audience listening, using automated n8n workflows to scrape and analyze Reddit sentiment with focused in-app campaigns across push notifications, billboards, and featured trays. Additionally, I automated playback tracking by linking Mixpanel data straight to Google Sheets via Apps Script, ensuring fast iteration on thumbnail creative selection and recommendations.

Outcome

Achieved 95% on-time campaign rollouts across 50+ in-app engagement pushes, reduced team reporting turn-around times by 60%, and significantly boosted content discovery and CTR. By evaluating drop-off rates and playback trends in real-time, I delivered actionable weekly performance insights that continuously optimized homepage merchandising and viewer retention strategies.
