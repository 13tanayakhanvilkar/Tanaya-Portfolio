The Challenge

Building a scalable alternative to enterprise GEO tools

When tasked with tracking our brand presence in Google AI Overviews, I quickly hit a wall with commercial options: enterprise tools were astronomically expensive, while budget alternatives had severe query limits that couldn't scale.

Rather than accepting a costly, manual research workflow, I built a custom end-to-end automation pipeline. The system automatically audits target queries to detect brand citations, identifies which competitors appear when we are missing, and triggers AI summaries revealing the exact content, entity, and schema gaps causing us to lag behind.
