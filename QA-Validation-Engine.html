The Challenge

The Status Quo & Operational Bottlenecks

During live sports ad delivery, IPL and World Cup campaigns required manual line-by-line Ctrl+F scans across raw JSON payloads. Non-technical QA teams were forced to inspect nested campaign code by hand while operating under broadcast deadlines and high campaign velocity.

This created a slow, fragile process. One small mismatch,a swapped title, an invisible trailing space, or a mobile character overflow could break UI formatting, delay launch, or disrupt the live experience. QA was running on effort, not systems.

Character Overflows

Titles truncating on mobile layouts during live delivery.

Swapped Fields

Descriptions pasted into title slots and breaking UI rendering.

Invisible Whitespaces

Trailing spaces breaking rendering regex and ad output.

Asset URL Verification

Opening image links manually wasted hours during tight launch windows.

The Automated System

Serverless Validation Engine

The solution combined a Google Apps Script parsing engine with an automated visual Google Sheets dashboard. It read multi-nested JSON campaign payloads, validated field logic, flagged invalid combinations, and served results in a structured, review-friendly layout.

Instead of asking engineers to inspect raw code manually, operations teams could work from a readable, color-coded dashboard that surfaced issues in real time. This kept live campaign QA fast, transparent, and scalable.

Automated Regex & Whitespace Sanitizer

Cleaned hidden errors before they reached the live ad payload.

Real-Time Character Cap Alerts

Flagged mobile overflow risks before launch or rendering.

In-Cell Thumbnail Renderer

Rendered asset previews directly inside the dashboard for instant visual checks.

Instant Pass/Fail Status Badges

Gave QA teams immediate signal without technical interpretation.

Impact

Faster QA, cleaner delivery, and zero live disruption.

The framework eliminated the bottleneck that was slowing down live sports campaigns. It enabled operations teams to validate payloads independently, reduced repeated engineering triage, and drastically lowered the risk of launch-day errors.

In practical terms, it saved hundreds of developer hours, removed dependence on technical review for routine validation, and supported seamless ad delivery during high-stakes moments.
